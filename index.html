What to check before ordering

  1. Confirm the exact variant. Match the color and size to the seller’s listing. Compare measurements with a garment or pair of shoes you already own.
  2. Estimate the delivered cost. Add domestic delivery, any agent fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties to the item price. Compare parcel quotes using the destination and estimated dimensions.
  3. Review quality-control photos. If your agent offers warehouse photos, compare them with your selected variant. Ask for a measurement or close-up if the label, stitching or sole is unclear.

Frequently asked questions

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference for comparing item links, prices and product details before placing an order through a shopping agent. This independent guide links to listings hosted by HipobuyFind.

Can I use an item with KakoBuy?

Open the HipobuyFind listing and look for its original seller link. Check whether KakoBuy accepts that marketplace and can load the exact variant before you pay.

Does the displayed price include shipping?

The catalog shows the listed item price. Obtain a separate quote for shipping and check the applicable fees before ordering.
